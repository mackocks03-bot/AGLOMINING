Our History

Tanzania has a thriving mining industry, and gold mining is a potential driver of economic development. Sound national development plans, world-class infrastructure, investor-friendly policies, and regulatory frameworks attract significant foreign investments to Tanzania. The nation's abundant natural resources and biodiversity attracted the founders of AFRIGOLD AND MINING INV. CO. LIMITED.

In 2026, recognizing that Tanzania is a land of endless opportunities for mining and investment, the promoters took a major step forward by entering the rapidly growing mining sector. AFRIGOLD AND MINING INV. CO. LIMITED's key project activity involves the extraction of high-quality gold bars and gold concentrates.

Who We Are

About Us

AFRIGOLD AND MINING INV. CO. LIMITED is a pioneering gold mining leader headquartered in Chunya, Mbeya, United Republic of Tanzania. Established in 2021, the company is founded on sustainable business philosophies intended to transform natural resources into tools for national development. Our state-of-the-art facility in Chunya includes exploration, mines, a laboratory, a chemical plant, stores, a workshop, processing plants, sales, logistics, and construction. The company has emerged as a leading player in the global mining industry by expanding its presence in gold mining and trading.

Our Vision

To be the most responsible and largest gold mining corporation in the world, focusing on sustainable human development.

Our Mission

To deliver incredible synergy and enduring value to the national economy and to humanity.

Health & Safety

🏥

Health

At AFRIGOLD, we prioritize the physical and mental well-being of our workforce. We believe a healthy team is the foundation of a productive mine. We provide on-site medical services, conduct regular health screenings, and implement robust programs to combat local health challenges, ensuring high standards of sanitation and workplace hygiene across all sites in Chunya.

⛑️

Safety

Safety is our license to operate. Our top-notch health and safety policies make our operations a safer place to work. We strive to maintain a secure mining industry by embedding a culture of safety and individual accountability at every level. Our business principles are based on targeting 'Zero Harm.' Our operations strictly adhere to the Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA 2003) of Tanzania.

Stewardship

Environmental Commitment

AFRIGOLD AND MINING INV. CO. LIMITED is committed to addressing environmental impacts and protecting the local ecosystem of Chunya, including diverse species of animals and plants. We have implemented international standards regarding the handling of hazardous materials. We ensure improved energy efficiency and increased productivity to minimize our ecological footprint. We utilize advanced filtration systems for treating and handling tailing materials before they are managed at designated sites.

Our People

National Workforce

🇹🇿 Tanzania First

In alignment with Tanzania's Local Content regulations, we prioritize the recruitment of talented Tanzanians. Since 2021, we have remained committed to the 'Tanzania First' principle. We actively recruit from Chunya and Mbeya, providing stable employment and facilitating the transfer of technical expertise to ensure a sustainable future for the Tanzanian mining industry.
